This experience uses your front camera to track hand gestures. Your camera feed stays on your device and is never recorded or transmitted.

Camera access is needed for this experience. You can enable it in your browser settings.

HOW TO INTERACT

✌️ SPREAD two fingers — move color sources
🤏 PINCH thumb + index — grab one color
👇 PINCH LOW near bar — browse palettes

swipe bar or tap COLORS to use touch controls

This experience requires WebGL2, which is not supported by your browser. Please try Safari 15+ or Chrome on a modern device.
